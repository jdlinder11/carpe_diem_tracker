Privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Summary

Carpe Diem does not have accounts and does not run a server that stores your habits. Everything you enter stays in your own browser on your own device.

What the tracker stores

The habits you add and the days you mark are saved in your browser's local storage (localStorage). This data:

  • stays on the device and browser where you entered it;
  • is not sent to us or to any server we operate;
  • is not shared between your devices.

You can delete it at any time with the "Erase everything" button at the bottom of the tracker, or by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. Because we never receive your data, we cannot recover it for you once it is deleted.

Hosting and fonts

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you load a page. Pages on this site also load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests font files from Google's servers. See GitHub's privacy statement and Google's privacy policy for how they handle this information.

Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit the site and which pages they read. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type. It does not receive the habits you enter in the tracker. You can learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Advertising

We may show advertising provided by Google AdSense. If we do:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland will be asked for consent before personalized ads or analytics cookies are used.

Affiliate links

Some pages may include affiliate links. If you buy something through one of these links, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
