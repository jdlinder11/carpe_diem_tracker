How to use it

  • Write the month at the top and your habits in the left column.
  • Fill in a square each day you complete a habit. A single dot, cross or full square all work.
  • Keep the sheet where you will see it daily, such as the fridge or beside your desk.
  • At the end of the month, count the filled squares in each row and decide which habits to keep.
