Habit ideas: 78 small habits to track

Every habit here is small and specific, so you know exactly when it is done. Pick one or two that serve something you care about, rather than the ones that sound most impressive.

Start with no more than three habits. You can add more once the first ones feel automatic.

Health and body

  • Drink a glass of water on waking
  • Walk 10 minutes after lunch
  • Stretch for 5 minutes
  • Take the stairs
  • Eat one serving of vegetables at lunch
  • Lights out by a set time
  • No screens for 30 minutes before bed
  • Stand up once an hour while working
  • Do 10 push-ups or squats
  • Step outside for daylight before 10 a.m.
  • Floss
  • Pack lunch the night before
  • Take a rest day on purpose

Mind and learning

  • Read 10 pages
  • Write one line in a journal
  • Learn five words in a new language
  • Meditate for 5 minutes
  • Listen to an educational podcast on the commute
  • Write down three things that went well
  • Practice an instrument for 15 minutes
  • Do one puzzle
  • Read one long article instead of headlines
  • Draw or sketch for 10 minutes
  • Write 200 words
  • Review your notes from the day
  • Spend 10 minutes outside without your phone

Home and order

  • Make the bed
  • Clear the kitchen counter before bed
  • Put away one item that is out of place
  • Run the dishwasher or wash up after dinner
  • Do one load of laundry from start to finish
  • Ten-minute tidy before bed
  • Water the plants
  • Take out recycling on schedule
  • Lay out tomorrow's clothes
  • Wipe down the bathroom sink
  • Open the mail the day it arrives
  • Plan tomorrow's meals
  • Empty the inbox tray on your desk

Work and focus

  • Write the day's top three tasks
  • Do the hardest task first
  • One 25-minute focus block with notifications off
  • Check email at set times only
  • Close all tabs at the end of the day
  • Write a short end-of-day note for tomorrow
  • Take a real lunch break
  • Reply to one message you have been avoiding
  • Spend 15 minutes on a long-term project
  • Update your task list
  • Leave work on time
  • Keep your phone in another room during focus time
  • Ask one question you have been putting off

Money

  • Record every purchase
  • Check your bank balance
  • No impulse purchases over a set amount
  • Move a small amount to savings
  • Bring coffee from home
  • Wait 24 hours before buying non-essentials
  • Review subscriptions once a week
  • Cook dinner at home
  • Write down one money goal for the week
  • Use cash for one spending category
  • Compare prices before a purchase
  • Pay a bill the day it arrives
  • Read one page about personal finance

People and relationships

  • Send one message to a friend
  • Call a family member
  • Give one genuine compliment
  • Eat one meal without screens
  • Ask someone a real question and listen
  • Write a thank-you note
  • Plan one thing to look forward to with someone
  • Put the phone away during conversations
  • Check in on a colleague
  • Remember one birthday this week
  • Say yes to one invitation
  • Do one small kind thing unasked
  • Share something you learned with someone

How to choose

  • Start from a goal. Ask what you want more of this year, then choose the smallest daily action that moves you toward it.
  • Prefer actions over outcomes. "Walk 10 minutes" is a habit. "Lose five pounds" is a result. Track the action.
  • Make it yours. Change the numbers to fit your life. Five minutes done every day beats thirty minutes done twice.
